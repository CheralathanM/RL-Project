How to play

Objective

25 rounds. End a round alone on the hill at C3 to score +1. If both players stand on it, nobody scores. Highest total after round 25 wins.

Actions

  • Move — one orthogonal step. Beats Hold.
  • Shoot — fire at any block. A hit knocks the target back to its corner. Beats Move.
  • Hold — stay put and block one incoming shot. Beats Shoot.

Both sides commit at the same time, then the round resolves.

Respawn

Scoring moves you off the hill into one of the four blocks around it — B3, D3, C2 or C4 — so a point cannot become permanent camping.

Shot restriction

You cannot target the same block on two consecutive shots. Shoot elsewhere first. The same rule binds the AI.

What the AI can see

Not your position. Only your action type, who took the hill, whether its shot hit, was shielded or missed, and that a respawn happened. It rebuilds a probability distribution over your location from that — press B to see it on the board.
