How to play

Goal

Ten turns. You score +1 for each turn you end alone on the hill at (2,2). If you both stand on it, nobody scores. Highest total wins.

Actions

  • Move — one orthogonal step. Beats Hold.
  • Shoot — fire at any cell. A hit sends the target back to spawn. Beats Move.
  • Hold — stay put and block one incoming shot. Beats Shoot.

Both sides commit simultaneously, then the turn resolves.

What the bot can see

Not your position. Only your action type, who took the hill, and whether its own shot hit, was shielded, or missed. It rebuilds a probability distribution over your location from that — press B to see it drawn on the board.
